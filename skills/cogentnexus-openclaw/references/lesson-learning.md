# Lesson Learning

Use after a meaningful failure, successful recovery, user correction, surprising result, repeated friction, or discovery likely to improve future work. Skip routine success, transient noise, speculation, and facts already captured.

## Purpose

Turn verified experience into a compact model of cause, correction, and future action. Capture useful reasoning outcomes without private chain-of-thought or a transcript.

## Workflow

1. **Trigger:** confirm the event has reusable value.
2. **Reconstruct:** use tool output, files, timestamps, or user feedback to establish what happened.
3. **Separate:** distinguish observation from diagnosis. Mark uncertain causes as hypotheses.
4. **Compare:** record the attempted action and result, then the correction and its verified result.
5. **Extract:** state the smallest reusable lesson and the condition where it applies.
6. **Prescribe:** define what to do first next time, what to avoid, and how to verify.
7. **Store:** append the structured record to the current daily memory. Promote only the distilled rule to `MEMORY.md` when it is durable.
8. **Deduplicate:** merge with an existing lesson when the cause and prescription are materially the same.
9. **Review:** revise or retire a lesson when later evidence contradicts it.

## Required record

Use [lesson-template.md](../assets/lesson-template.md). Preserve these semantics even when writing more compactly:

- **Situation and objective:** what was being attempted and the observable success criterion.
- **Action:** what was actually tried, including relevant configuration or constraints.
- **Observed result:** verified outcome; never intended outcome.
- **Diagnosis:** supported cause, or explicitly labeled hypothesis.
- **Correction:** what changed and why.
- **Correction result:** evidence after the change; use `not yet verified` when applicable.
- **Lesson:** a reusable cause-and-effect rule, including scope or conditions.
- **Next time:** first action, avoidance rule, and verification check.
- **Confidence and evidence:** high/medium/low plus concise evidence pointers.
- **Tags/date:** retrieval terms and when the lesson was learned.

## Quality rules

- Prefer causal statements over chronology.
- Keep failed attempts only when they explain the lesson.
- Do not claim a fix worked without post-change evidence.
- One record should teach one main lesson; split unrelated findings.
- Never store secrets, raw private reasoning, or full logs.
- A negative result is still useful when it narrows the cause.
- If no correction succeeded, say so and record the safest next experiment.
